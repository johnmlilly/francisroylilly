Francis continues to progress really well and make small improvements each day! He has continued to be able to handle increased feedings as well as the transition from a cannula to a CPAP.

The EEG was put back on his head on Tuesday evening through early Wednesday morning and unfortunately it detected multiple sub clinical seizures. It did not meet the criteria for status epilepticus, which is good. His doctors do not recommend putting him back on the heavier medications or reintubating him since he has made a lot of positive progress overall. However, they are concerned about his seizures and how we can manage them for the future. They are trying different combinations of medications to see what works the best and will be putting the EEG back on him starting tomorrow.

We were very discouraged and anxious when we received the news about the seizures yesterday. We had in some sense expected it but had hoped he was past them given how well he has improved in such a short time. We continue to trust in the Lord and His healing power.

The doctors have spoken to us about the long term plan to get Francis home. They are still optimistic and hopeful we can get there even in spite of the seizures. One of the other things they are looking for him to do is swallow on his own since this is an indication for whether or not he will be able to feed on his own. We haven't seen him swallow yet but we are confident he can learn to do it given more time and some therapy.
