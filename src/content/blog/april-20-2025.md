It is resurrection Sunday, indeed! Please storm Heaven with your prayers for the next 24 hours and in particular between 2-3pm today.

We received a call from one of Francis' doctors as the girls were finishing their Easter egg hunt. She shared with us that Francis is doing so much better than she ever expected after his sedation and it is one of the most remarkable things she's seen in her career. She believes he is now ready to extubate and will do so in the next hour or so!

Please pray that the extubation is successful and he breaths well on his own and that the Lord continues breathing life into him this blessed and remarkable day of the resurrection.
