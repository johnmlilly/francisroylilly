In our last update, we asked to pray specifically “For his brain to grow larger and show through his head shape”. Well, Francis went to his first follow-up neurologist appointment on June 27. His outpatient neurologist that was assigned to us is actually one of the first doctors that had seen him and watched him closely in the first couple weeks of his life so she knows him very well. When she saw him, she was over the moon to see how well he is doing. In fact, she is the first doctor that he smiled at. We are convinced it is because he knows her. We expected to talk about his head shape and the concern for his brain growth but his neurologist said, “Wow, his head looks so much better. His brain is definitely growing.”

God is good and He hears our exact prayers.

Francis is 3 months old as of last Tuesday. I actually got some milestone photos done and finally edited photos of him that I've taken on my professional camera. You can see that he is clearly engaging when he is awake and alert. He loves it when we talk to him and he is smiling and cooing. He’s started to notice his toys more, he loves his pacifier, and he is a pro at bottle and breast feeding! We are so hopeful that he will continue improving and eventually fully feed through mouth.

Francis has four developmental assessments expected this month with different doctors and therapists. We should have a good sense of his delays and the potential of a cerebral palsy diagnosis very soon. The primary concerns are his lack of movement and his “high muscle tone” noticed by his neurologist. Essentially he has tightened muscles that may indicate his brain is not communicating for his gross motor skills to develop properly. However, due to early intervention through therapies, there is very good chance he will be able to improve and overcome some of the adversities he may face in the future.

One of the most eye-opening parts of all of this has been the honor of hearing from other parents who have children with a similar story to Francis. I am blown away by the strength of these parents and new friends of mine. John and I are so humbled by the amount of support we have received just because of our willingness to share. When you think of us, please remember and pray for all parents and children who have suffered from traumatic births.

The other day, I was reflecting on the Gospel reading of the day:

> “People do not put new wine into old wineskins. Otherwise the skins burst, the wine spills out, and the skins are ruined. Rather, they pour new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved.” - Matthew 9:17

It reminded me that God makes “ALL things new” (Revelation 21:5). We are learning everyday what our new season of life looks like and how to embrace it fully in order to receive every grace from God possible. Please pray for us that we continue to rely fully on God’s grace.

Prayer Requests:

- For Francis’ high muscle tone to improve and to respond well to his therapies
- For Francis’ developmental assessments that he witnesses to God’s power by being more capable than expected
- For all parents and children who have suffered and are suffering from traumatic births
