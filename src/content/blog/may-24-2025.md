Thank you for your prayers this past Wednesday when Francis had his surgery. The surgeon said it went very well. Francis woke up from his sedation and was extubated after only a few hours, allowing me to hold him to sleep before I had to leave for the evening.

He was able to resume feedings that night and was back to full feedings through his new g-tube and limited bottle and breast feeding the next day.

John and I are quickly learning how to use the feeding equipment so that we can fully care for Francis as soon as he gets home. We are now in preparation to be discharged and having all of the conversations with his doctors and therapists about what is next. It is all overwhelming but also so exciting to be able to look forward to this new chapter after all that we've been through.

Most notably, one of Francis' neurologists came to check on him while we were away and according to his nurse, she was moved emotionally to see how well he is doing. We have now heard from several doctors and nurses that they believe what happened was a miracle.

There is still uncertainty about how Francis will develop but we are so confident that God has a great plan in store for him and our family.

I also wanted to give a couple of updates on the other babies you may have been praying for. Thomas is home and could use continued prayers as him and his parents navigate many upcoming follow-up appointments. To respect their privacy, I won't share much but please continue praying for Cecilia, Estavan, and their families during their NICU stay and beyond.

Finally we ask for your prayers over the next several days as we prepare for Francis to come home!

Prayer requests:

- For peace and guidance as we transition to bringing Francis home
- For Bella and Maxine as they learn and adjust to being older sisters
- For Francis' overall healing and development and his life to continue
- For Thomas, Cecilia, Estavan, and their families
