Francis Roy is officially three weeks old and we hit his original due date!

While the last three weeks have been the hardest of our lives, we would never trade this time we have had with him. The miracle we witnessed on Sunday would not have impacted us in the same way if we did not completely abandon ourselves to the uncertainty of God's will and learned to trust him in a way we never have before.

By now you have probably heard about the passing of Pope Francis. Some friends have shared the thought that Pope Francis' death may have given way for our precious Francis' life. We will never know whether that's true or not but a friend shared a passage from a book that featured letters from children who had questions for Pope Francis. You can see in the photos the pages of the book that lead us to now ask him to intercede and pray for our Francis along with the many saints we've shared about.

Francis is doing really well breathing on his own. They continue to be able to wean the pressures off his respiratory support. He is also increasing feeds through his feeding tube and can tolerate 25 mls now which means he's more than halfway to a regular feeding for his age and weight.

His care team which includes his neonatologist, neurologist, and nurses all agreed to put the EEGs back on today to gather data and information with the purpose of knowing what care he needs moving forward if he is still having subclinical seizures.

We have been really moved by all of you and your fervent prayers. We are so so grateful that you continue to pray for Francis and our continued journey ahead.

Prayer requests:

- For his complete and miraculous healing
- For no more seizures, both subclinical and clinical
- For successful weaning off all of his respiratory support
- For peace, wisdom, and guidance from the Holy Spirit during our meeting with his doctors
