The past few days have been a rollercoaster to say the least. We received the news about Francis' final prognosis on Tuesday and the doctors and nurses began preparing all of us for his final days.

They allowed the girls to have another special visit with him so we could be together as a family as much as possible. We made foot and hand molds with Francis and Bella was granted her wish to hold her baby brother. She was asked twice if she wanted to put him back down and she asked to hold for longer both times. It was so special to see how careful and nurturing she was with him. She's been asking to hold him again since then.

With everything going on, we weren't able to attend Holy Thursday Mass like we have in the past so John and I thought it would be special to wash Francis' feet as Jesus did for his disciples. It was a very sacred moment for us in the midst of everything.

We decided to each spend a night with Francis in the NICU and "keep vigil" with him. John spent Thursday night and at this point the doctors had weaned Francis off of the heavy seizure medications that had been sedating him.

We were mentally prepared to see very little brain activity and responsiveness when he woke up since our understanding is that his injury has worsened. However, when he began to fully awake Friday morning, we noticed much more than expected. Francis had fully opened both eyes, blinking, had a startle reflex, and even coughed during one of our care times. At this point, we felt it was important to talk to his doctor to ask if he has more of a chance for improving than we thought.

We told his doctor that our instinct was to give him more time and she agreed that we should. That said, we are again taking it one day at a time. He is still in critical condition but it seems that our little lion continues to keep everyone on their toes. He has shown small signs of respiratory drive and he has been able to hold a normal amount of oxygen for a shortly sustained period.

We are hopeful that God may be granting our miracle but we are focusing on surrendering each moment to the Lord and trusting in Him throughout this whole journey with Francis.

Please keep these specific requests in your prayers:

- That he is completely healed from all subclinical seizure activity and he does not experience clinical seizures
- That his brain to kidney functions normalize and he absorbs more fluids in his system
- That his brain to lung/respiratory functions continue improving so that he may breath completely on his own
- That he is comforted by the Lord and our mother, Mary, through any discomfort he may be experiencing
- For the gifts of courage and discernment as we continue working with his care team
