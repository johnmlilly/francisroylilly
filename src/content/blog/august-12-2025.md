On Saturday, July 12, surrounded by our family and so many close friends who have been praying for us, we officially welcomed Francis into the church. It was a day we never thought would have been possible when he was born on April 1. We felt an outpouring of joy and gratitude at the many faces we saw smiling back at us in the church. These are the people - plus so many more! - that we wish could have been present and who have carried us in all of this. They remind us of the reality we do not see. God working miracles in our midst.

During the ceremony, the first reading was taken from Ezekiel 47 and spoke about an angel of the Lord showing Ezekiel something astonishing. The angels shows him fresh water flowing from the temple of the Lord - which represents the new waters of baptism - that transformed the salt water of the Dead Sea. There was abundant life in a place where no one else thought there could be, where nothing could sustain life. God finds a way, and works a miracle. This reading was the same one on the day Francis was born, April 1! God brought life to him where no one thought there could be. God healed him bit by bit in ways that confounded everyone's expectations.

Francis hit 4 months of life just a few weeks ago! Since our last update, he has had four different developmental assessments with various doctors and therapists. Each appointment has been a bit of a rollercoaster. On July 23rd, he had his follow-up appointment with his rehabilitation doctor. At the last appointment, his doctor concluded that there was “high suspicion” of cerebral palsy. Heading into this appointment, Kara felt called to ask for a storm of prayers to grant a miracle of “no suspicion of cerebral palsy”. At the appointment, his doctor concluded from the various assessments that she was comfortable giving him an early diagnosis of CP. We still do not know the full severity of the diagnosis. However, the next day at his follow-up assessment for developmental delays, the occupational therapist shared that she did not see the normal outward signs of CP from him. Perhaps this was another answer to our prayers of “no suspicion of CP”. Additionally, his Bailey assessment indicated that he is average in cognitive development, and only slightly delayed in language and motor. This was very encouraging.

We are still processing and learning more about his diagnosis. At the moment, it is primarily a way to get access to more resources as he develops. Our care plan for him goes unchanged though - he will begin many therapies to ensure the best chances for his future!

It has been easy to get caught up in what the doctors/therapists say when they are “concerned” but it reminds us how we need to keep praying since God always has the final word. We know that God still has an incredible plan for Francis' little life and that we are blessed to be a part of that.

Prayer requests:

- For his miraculous healing to be a witness to every person that’s meant to meet him
- For his head strength and control
- For his muscle tone to continue improving overall
- For continued improvement with bottle and breast feeding so that he passes his swallow study on September 9th
