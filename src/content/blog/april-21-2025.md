We are truly in awe of what the Lord has done over the last few days.

First off, thank you all so much for your prayers yesterday for Francis. His breathing tube was successfully removed and he has been doing great breathing on his own! They have had to adjust his pressure slightly several times but the drive to breathe is there, which was our biggest concern.

He does not have the EEG on his head to monitor brain activity, and while there is some concern with subclinical seizures, the neurologist is comfortable with keeping it off of him for now based on how well he has progressed. They will be keeping a close eye on his activity over the next several days to ensure he has no clinical seizures.

The timing of all of this is truly miraculous. From the evening of Holy Thursday through Easter Sunday, Francis has made a remarkable turnaround. It's clearly showing God's hand in all of this and we feel like Jesus is smiling down on us.

We also want to ask for prayers for another baby in the NICU, Thomas Cox. They have been praying through Thomas Vander Woude, a father who couragesouly sacrificed his life for his son. We have been praying to him to save Francis since we heard Thomas' story earlier last week. Please pray for Thomas and Francis through the intercession of Thomas Vander Woude through the following prayer that was shared with us:

> O Lord, Your Servant, Thomas Vanderwoude, didst truly follow your words when you taught that "there is no greater love than this: to lay down one's life for his friends". We ask You, therefore, through the intercession of Your exemplar servant for the complete miraculous healing of Thomas Cox and Francis Roy Lilly and that all our lives be worthy of Your Redemptive love. And if dying is also asked of us, grant us the necessary graces to imitate Your faithful servant. We ask this through the intercession of all Your Angels, Saints, and Faithful Departed. Amen.

Prayer Intentions

- For his seizures and for the doctors to continue to treat him
- For the complete healing of his brain
- For his ability to start swallowing
- For the continued ability to breathe on his own
- For Thomas Cox and his family's intentions
- For the conversion of all those who are witnessing Francis' life in the NICU
