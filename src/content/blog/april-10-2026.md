We have so much to be grateful for this Easter. The start of the month was Francis’ birthday, something we never thought possible this time last year. The next day, we entered into the Triduum which brought back many memories and emotions of our preparation to say goodbye from last year. Entering into the passion of Jesus in the most intense way imaginable. Experiencing the pain and suffering of the Father offering His son for us, surrendering Him over for our sake. Completely and totally. His only son. And then the miracle of Easter a few days later, when Francis began taking his own breath. The breath of life that Christ himself took that Easter morning as he made his triumphant comeback.

We are still processing and working through how to share the incredible experiences we’ve had. Sharing not just our personal accounts but the story of God’s providence and goodness and how we’ve witnessed it firsthand. We know we aren’t required to share anything but how can we not after all that God has done for us? We don’t want to forget what he’s done for our family and hope we can continue to remember it and turn towards Him in thanksgiving.

Happy Easter from all of us, and happy birthday to our beloved Francis 😊
