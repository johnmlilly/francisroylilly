Francis continues to make steady progress each day! He was given the green light to start bottle feeding a week ago today, and since then has made incredible progress. Over the last few days, he has consistenly taken between 10-20 milileters of milk out of his 75ml feed. The most he took at one time was 25ml, which is a story in and of itself. For context, to go home, he needs to take roughly 80% of that consistently for them to discharge us. The thing they will need to do is a swallow study to ensure he's not aspirating any fluid into his lungs. If he is, then he will automatically need a feeding tube to avoid fluid build up in his lungs. We are praying that is not the case and that God will continue to grant us a full and miraculous healing of our boy.

There was also some concern for craniosynostosis (early fusion of the skull bones) but an ultrasound revealed that this is not the case. We are still somewhat concerned with his head shape and how his brain might be growing and developing, but we continue to trust God with every aspect of Francis' life.

We also had an awesome moment of grace on Tuesday. Right after we had Kara's 6-week follow up appointment (which praise God she has made a complete recovery!), we went to the hospital discouraged and unsure of the week since we had both not seen Francis in some time. As we walked across the bridge to the hospital, we ran into Fr. Philip Briggs who was visiting several parishioners. He asked if he could accompany us for a quick visit to Francis. He said a prayer for a sick child and offered the Eucharist to us. He only had one host left with him, which is how we knew he was meant to be there with us. We felt encouraged and it reminded us of the early days with Francis and how many moments God has showed up for us.

These are our prayer requests this week:

- For his complete and miraculous healing that he might be a witness to all those that encounter him
- For him to fully pass his swallow study and not aspirate
- For him to continue to improve with bottle and breast feeding
- For his brain growth and development
- For another NICU mom, Carly, and her baby Cecilia
