Reflecting back on the first week of Francis' life, I remember sincerely begging God for His mercy on Francis and our family. This was the first time outside of the Mass that I spoke, "Lord, have mercy" and the first time I said it from my heart. The celebration of Divine Mercy Sunday this past weekend hit me deeply and reminded me that while we may beg for God's mercy, He does not grant it in a patronizing way but He grants it out of love and a desire to help us as His children. I am confident that that is why John and I found the Divine Mercy prayer card on Palm Sunday - to remind us of Jesus' mercy and love for us throughout all of this.

While we have been reminded many times over the past week that Francis is a "miracle baby", John and I have struggled to fully trust in that and surrender daily due to the many unknowns we still have ahead of us. We relate to the apostles who did not believe the resurrection accounts of others before they saw the risen Jesus themselves. Again, we are grateful for the Lord's mercy that He is there and will show up even when we don't believe.

We look back on the past several days and we have been able to see how generous the Lord is in answering our prayers. As Francis' nurse said, he has made many "impressive" improvements in just a week:

- He successfully weaned off of the first two phases of respiratory support and continues to handle each wean daily.
- He has increased to full milk feedings through his feeding tube which allowed them to remove his IV fluids.
- He has shown interest in his pacifier and showing signs of swallowing.
- He has graduated to a big boy crib since he is able to regulate his own body temperature.
- He took his first tub bath and can now wear clothes!!!

Our biggest concern moving forward continues to be the impact of his seizures. Since there is no way for us to know if he is regularly having subclinical seizures without the EEG monitor back on, we have really had to surrender and trust that the Lord is taking care of Francis in this aspect.

We are so grateful for the amazing staff and team at Inova Fairfax. Occupational and physical therapists are working with Francis daily to work on his mobility since he is at high-risk for cerebral palsy. We have been learning from them so that we can continue his exercises at each of his care times. This has been a really beautiful way to bond since he is so active and alert when we interact with him.

We are so moved and encouraged by all of your continual prayers. Our specific prayer requests at this time are:

- For him to successfully wean off of all respiratory support
- For no more seizures, both subclinical or clinical, and for his previous seizures to have had minimal impact
- For his ability to properly coordinate sucking and swallowing so that he can begin to bottle and breast feed over the next few weeks
- For peace and guidance as John and I continue navigating this as parents
- For Francis' NICU friends - Thomas, Cecilia, and Estavan
