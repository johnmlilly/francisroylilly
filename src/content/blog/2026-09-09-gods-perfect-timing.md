We apologize for leaving you all hanging after our last update and prayer request.

Your prayers meant so much to us that day since the day of his surgery ended up being another story to add to our journey.

The morning of his surgery, I was feeling rather anxious since this would be the first time we were going into the same hospital that he had spent 2 months in the NICU. While I was tempted to distract myself with random music and podcasts, I decided to play my daily prayer routine on the Hallow app as I drove us very early to his appointment.

I normally listen to the daily gospel and reflections that are provided and it happened to the be the Feast of St. Bonaventure who is known as one of the Church’s greatest theologians. One fact that I was not aware of until that very morning was that St. Bonaventure was deathly ill as a child and his mother prayed through the intercession of St. Francis of Assisi for him to be healed. Miraculously, he was healed and eventually joined the Franciscan order when he was a young adult.  
I couldn’t believe what I had heard. It was a clear consolation from the Lord reminding me that I had prayed for my son’s health before and the miracle that God did was in fact real and this minor surgery was going to be ok. 

Little did I know that that moment in prayer would give me strength for later in the day when Francis was admitted into the hospital for observation due to the possibility of aspiration during surgery. Seeing him coughing and groggy after surgery in his hospital bed brought back memories that were hard to relive. I felt comforted knowing that my little Francis was being prayed for by the many saints that have been praying for him since the beginning. 

He showed his strength once again to the doctors and we were able to go home later that evening. The actual surgery to enclose his g-tube hole went very smoothly and allowed him to move much more comfortably! While we had hoped to avoid surgery again, we are grateful that it was done with perfect timing and allowed our little guy to enjoy the rest of summer including his first time floating in the pool. Thank you to Dr. Hartwich and the many nurses and doctors who took great care ensuring he was safe and healthy to go home.

Most recently, Francis received his first pair of braces. They are the cutest little things and are helping stay supported while in standing. We aren’t sure how long he will need them but they are helping at the moment! We have also been able to get all of his therapies sorted out since moving to our new home. He is loving his new therapists and we were very grateful to get him scheduled again for the Richmond Hope Intensive. He started on August 24th and is finishing up this Friday.  
He has been making incremental progress towards crawling and standing on his own. It is hard to announce bigger milestones because he is slowly getting things in his own time. Before we realize it, he is learning new things and it’s just a normal skill! We’re excited to see that come true for crawling and standing as well. 

It is hard to be patient when you know your child is so capable and has such great potential. I try to remind myself that it is also a gift to see them work so hard and achieve the smallest steps that lead to the biggest victories. I could use prayers for that since I know that is likely how God sees us and yet He doesn’t get frustrated. He is so patient with us as we fumble our way through life until we grow in the ways He made us to become.

All of that said, we would love continued prayers for Francis as he finishes up his intensive this week! 

Thank you from the bottom of our hearts for continuing on this journey with us. We feel your prayers always and could not be more grateful for the incredibly community you have all become.

**TLDR: Francis’s g-tube enclosure surgery was successful and has allowed him to make great progress towards gross motor development; God made himself known the day of surgery through the intercession of St. Bonaventure; Francis is currently participating in his therapy intensive again and received braces to help him in standing!**
