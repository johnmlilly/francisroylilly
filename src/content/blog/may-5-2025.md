Last Thursday marked one whole month with our sweet Francis! We are celebrating each little step forward—and there have been some BIG ones lately.

For his one month birthday, Francis was given the gift of room air! He is completely off respiratory support—thank you, Lord! Breathing completely on his own is a huge milestone, and we are beyond grateful. The joys have continued as he wore his Star Wars Day outfit, the sisters got to have another visit, and we celebrated the third Sunday of Easter. I read a reflection to Francis from Divine Intimacy that I'd love to share here:

> "I hope for much from Your bounty, O Lord, because You Yourself teach us to ask, to seek, and to knock; at Your word, I ask, I seek, I knock. O Lord, You who tell us to ask, grant that I may receive; You who tell us to seek, grant that I may find; You who teach us to knock at the door, open to the one who is knocking!"

And so now we ask God to help him overcome the next big hurdle: feeding. We're waiting on the speech pathologist to evaluate Francis and hopefully give him the green light to begin bottle feeding. Please join us in praying boldly for this next miracle—that his little body will be ready, and that he will make substantial progress. It will truly be a miracle if he can suddenly take off with feeding, and we know that with God, nothing is impossible.

Thank you for walking this journey with us. We continue in confidence that the Lord hears all of our prayers.
