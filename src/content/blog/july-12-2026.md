We have another update and an urgent prayer request!

First off, we are giving thanks today since it’s the anniversary of officially welcoming Francis into the Church and celebrating his Baptism (not his actual baptism anniversary—he was baptized on his birthday by Fr. Peter Clem). It’s also our daughter Bella’s actual baptism anniversary, as well as his cousin Ethan’s 5th birthday, who was also in the NICU.

This date holds a very special meaning for us, but also because it is the Feast Day of Sts. Zelie and Louis Martin. We developed a devotion to them early in our marriage because of their incredible faithfulness as a married couple and as parents. Through their faith and love, they helped form their daughter, St. Thérèse, into the saint we know today. We named Bella Therese in her honor.

With that said, it was not planned for either Bella's or Francis’s baptism celebrations to land on July 12 (five years apart), since that was simply the date available at two different parishes at the time. We believe that was by design. It has brought us so much comfort to be reminded each year of the promise we made as parents: to raise them as faithful children of the Church, knowing how deeply loved they are, and that they are called to be great witnesses in the world.

As we mentioned in our last update, we were hoping that Francis’s G-tube site would heal on its own. The surgeon determined at his appointment last week that surgery is necessary to fully close the hole and prevent any further leakage and infection.

Francis has surgery scheduled for this Wednesday to close his G-tube site. We are a bit nervous because it will require Francis to undergo general anesthesia, but the procedure itself is only about 30 minutes, and he should be able to go home the same day. The surgery is scheduled for 8:30 a.m. on July 15, so if you could say a special prayer for him then, we would be so grateful.

We also have good news to share! We were approved for financial assistance at Inova for his weekly therapies. This will finally allow him to get back to regular therapy during this very critical window for development. We’re extremely grateful for all of your prayers.

We’ve been thinking recently about how incredible it is that almost every time we share our prayer intentions, they seem to be answered so quickly. We continue to discern what, how, and when we should share our intentions because we never want to treat the Lord like a vending machine. However, when we feel convicted to share, it seems as though it’s not only for our own prayers to be answered, but maybe also to invite others to deepen their trust in the Lord.

We’ve had several people ask if our GoFundMe is still active and if we’re still accepting donations. Our link is still open and is linked on our CaringBridge site as well as here: [https://gofund.me/7c58552d9](https://gofund.me/7c58552d9)

We are always so grateful, and we truly believe that the Lord has provided for us every step of the way. If you choose to donate, the funds will primarily go toward therapies that aren’t covered by financial assistance while we wait for the Medicaid waiver, as well as counseling services for our family as we continue to recover from the trauma of this past year.

Prayer request: For a smooth G-tube closure surgery and quick recovery for Francis this Wednesday, July 15 at 8:30 a.m.!
