Francis has a swallow study coming up this Tuesday at 8:45am. This is a big appointment because it will help determine whether he can start weaning off his feeding tube, or if he’ll need it a while longer until we introduce solids. One of his therapists recently told us that she’s confident he’ll be able to take solids, which is such an encouraging sign!

Right now, the main reason he still needs the G-tube is because he was silently aspirating during his first study in the NICU. He can still breastfeed and drink from a bottle, but he’s limited to just 30ml (about 1 oz) at a time. The hardest part is that he loves the bottle and gets so upset when we cut him off.

We’re so grateful for the G-tube, though, because it allowed us to bring him home from the hospital much sooner than we otherwise could have. When he first failed the swallow study, Kara and I were disappointed since we had been hoping it would be another sign of his miraculous healing. But God has shown us again and again that He works through all things. Our care team reminded us that feeding is often the biggest hurdle for NICU babies and can keep them in the hospital for weeks or even months. Looking back, we’re thankful Francis could come home sooner, and since then we’ve seen such steady growth and progress. Bella and Max absolutely adore him and are so sweet with him every day.

The feeding tube has its challenges and it’s hard to see him upset when the bottle runs out. We’re hopeful that the extra time and practice have made him stronger, and we’re praying that this swallow study will be the step he needs to move forward.

Please keep Francis in your prayers this coming Tuesday—that he passes this study with flying colors and that we continue to trust in God’s will no matter the outcome.
