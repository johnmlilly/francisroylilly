We entered the hospital both nervous and hopeful. It is bitter sweet anytime we pass or go by the NICU - a place where we have experienced so much fear and pain but also incredible hope, healing, and care.

Francis’s NICU neonatologist Dr. Hwang joined us for the study and we were so happy to have a familiar face and one of his biggest cheerleaders by our side! This day has been a long time coming for everyone.

Kara was able to feed the bottle to Francis while John and Dr. Hwang watched on the monitors. Francis seemed to be doing well with the ultra-preemie bottle he has been on for the past few months. They changed the nipple to preemie and finally to a transitional nipple. Every time they changed, Francis would let it be known that he wanted the bottle back!

Afterwards, we learned that Francis did NOT aspirate while using the ultra-preemie and even the preemie nipples! He did aspirate on the transitional which was not a huge surprise since he is not used to that fast of a milk flow.

Our prayers have once again been answered and we are again in shock and awe of the Lord. He PASSED!

Francis has now been given the green light to use a preemie nipple with his bottles which will hopefully help him be more efficient as we begin increasing his milk volume through bottle.

He will continue using his g-tube since it will take time before he is able to fully take his bottle by mouth but we know he is capable!!!

We appreciate all prayers and encouragement as he moves forward. We are hopeful that our little lion will be able to get his tube removed in no time so he can enjoy his bottles and eventually purées and yummy solids too!
