"But now, thus says the LORD, who created you, Jacob, and formed you, Israel: Do not fear, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by name: you are mine." - Isaiah 43:1

Last Tuesday was John and my 8 year wedding anniversary. We spent most of the day with Francis but we were grateful for a friend's help so that we were able to get out a little bit and celebrate. We ended up spending some time in prayer at our local perpetual adoration chapel. We realized that the last time we had prayed there was when we decided on Francis' name.

We weren't sure about sharing this story here but it seems so relevant at the moment. John and I always had a different name in mind for our firstborn son until the day we got home from my 20 week prenatal appointment. We shared with Bella that she was going to have a baby brother and after 20 weeks of her coming up with silly names that would never work, she replied "Yay, his name is Francis!" John and I turned to each other and thought, "where'd that come from"? We had never thought of the name Francis and we were pretty certain Bella didn't know anyone named Francis.

That said, the name really stuck with us and we decided to take it to prayer like we had with both Bella and Maxine's names. We went to adoration and walked out feeling very at peace and certain that the name of the little human growing inside me was Francis Roy. Francis which means "free man" and Roy which means "King".

You can imagine that when we heard Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost was elected to be pope, we thought it was another interesting connection much like the timing of Pope Francis' passing. However, when we heard that he would be named Pope Leo XIV, we truly felt that God was affirming the inspiration of his name. The name Leo means "Lion" which we have been referring to Francis as our "little lion.". It is no mistake that lions are known as the "king" of the jungle. This was all a comforting nod and reminder of the promise God has for Francis in Isaiah 43:1.

Last week was all about getting Francis in a place to begin bottle feeding. All week we had been spending time helping him practice sucking on the pacifier and latching to breastfeed. All the while, the daily gospel readings from Sunday to Friday were themed around "hunger" and Jesus being the "bread of life".

"Jesus said to the crowds, "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me will never hunger, and whoever believes in me will never thirst." John 6:35

Our prayer for him has been that Francis will be able to "taste the goodness of the Lord" through the healing of his brain function that allows him to properly feed. It has been so encouraging to hear that many of you have prayed specifically for him to learn to suck and swallow.

Friday afternoon, we got a glimpse of our answered prayer as our doctor put orders in for him to begin bottle feeding. Francis then began to consistently suck the pacifier and successfully took small amounts of milk from the bottle over 24 hours.

For the first time, John and I heard our sweet boy fully cry out most likely out of hunger. Then on Saturday, he breastfed for the first time! Nursing my baby for even just a short time on the eve of Mother's Day was the sweetest gift.

His feeding ability continues to be a brain function concern but he is showing his ability to learn even if it will take time. There is a good chance that he will still need to go home with a gastrointestinal feeding tube but we are hopeful that he will learn faster than expected. I have been praying primarily through the intercession of Our Lady of La Leche for his feeding journey.

Additionally, we have these specific prayer requests as we enter a new week of life with him:

- For his complete and miraculous healing that he might be a witness to all those that encounter him
- For his "coordination" in bottle and breast feeding
- For the physical growth and development of his brain and for the sutures in his skull to mold back into place
- For peace and grace in navigating family life as John starts working again this week
