Thank you so much for your constant prayers. We feel them when we are with Francis and we feel them when we are not with him. Your prayers truly hold us up during this difficult time.

We received devastating news from the doctor yesterday that they believe they have done everything they can at this point. Francis' continued seizures indicate that his brain injury is only worsening and the last MRI showed that his brain cells are dying.

We are preparing to say goodbye to him soon and are leaning every bit on the Lord during this Holy Week.

We ask that you please continue praying for a miracle since we know God can do anything but we also pray for peace and trust in Him with whatever He wills.

Francis was baptized and confirmed on his birthday, April 1st by our dear and closest friend, Fr. Peter Clem.

We are so grateful for the time we have had with him and the time we have left to make more memories and to hold him as much as possible. We know this is a gift and we praise God for that in spite of the pain we are experiencing.
