The Lord is never outdone in generosity. Back in February, our update stated:

> “We’re also looking into Richmond Hope Therapy, a weeks-long intensive therapy center that we believe could significantly help his development and overall trajectory. We’ve heard many positive things about this program and hope to be scheduled at the perfect time for his development.”

One of our prayer intentions was for:

> “Openings that work with our schedule at Richmond Hope Therapy”

At that time, we were told to expect an 8 month waiting period, Francis likely would not get into until the end of the year.

A few weeks ago, we received a call from Richmond Hope Therapy that there was a last-minute cancellation and they had an opening for Francis to start a three week therapy intensive on April 20. Although this meant we would have many logistics to sort out, John and I felt convicted that it was divine timing considering April 20 was the anniversary of Francis’s Easter Sunday miracle when he was found breathing on his own and extubated the same day. That day gave us so much hope and we felt it was aligning that Richmond Hope Therapy would bring us much of the same emotion. Once again, God hears our specific prayer. Thank you, Lord!

As I write this, I am sitting in Francis’s and my room at the Reinhart Guest House in Richmond. He will complete his second full week of therapy tomorrow afternoon and then we’ll head home for the weekend and do the daily commute to finish up his third week starting Monday, May 4.

Here at Richmond Hope, his main goals over the three weeks are to work on transitional movements, rolling over without assistance, and bearing weight through his legs, knees, and feet. He goes to OT and PT for one hour each every day Monday through Friday to work on these goals. He has been having so much fun, always getting his therapists to smile. He’s also been working incredibly hard as he’s really pushed through some difficult exercises. I have never been more proud to be his mama. I’ve gotten teary-eyed several times watching how much he rises to every challenge and shows all of us how capable he is.

Life has been very busy since our move and it’s been hard to remember what we’ve shared at this point but Francis has made some amazing progress. He began to sit up independently in early March and has only gotten stronger and more stable since then.

Another huge update is that he graduated feeding (speech) therapy on April 6th! He has been cleared to eat all foods and no longer needs to follow along with the Inova Fairfax Rehab Speech therapist since leaving the NICU last year. He still has his G-tube button placed, however, we expect it to be removed very soon!

His most recent medical appointment this past week was with his neurologist. She was so impressed by his progress and development and happy to hear that he is continuing to improve. Due to this, we made the decision to begin weaning him off of his seizure medication that we kept him on since the NICU. It will be a slow process and a bit scary since we will need to be vigilant of any seizure activity throughout. We would appreciate prayers for this over the next couple of months.

Since he has been fully cleared to eat all different solids, Francis had the BEST time getting to eat birthday cake multiple times throughout the month of April. One of his doctors said at his birthday party, “It’s a miracle alone that he’s eating his birthday cake.” And boy, did he eat it! I wish I had gotten a video of John bringing the cake down to him and Francis going for his cake with both hands as we sang the last “you” of Happy Birthday. It was a sweet moment that I don’t think any of us will forget.

Francis’s birthday celebrations were spread out from the end of March through the end of April. We also celebrated Easter and his sister Bella’s birthday. It was truly a month of joy. We had the opportunity to celebrate with so many of the people who stood by us the first 2 months of his life including his NICU nurses and doctors. It has been a gift to continue updating them with his progress and to share how much he’s grown and thrived because of the care they gave him. We are so proud to now call them not only Francis’s care team but also our friends.

Looking back on this year has given me so much hope and consolation in the Lord's will for Francis's life and our family's future, though not without its struggles and crosses. John and I have learned that while we can trust in the bigger plans God has for our lives, we must also trust Him in the small, everyday graces that carry us through the seemingly smaller obstacles. I've come to understand that this is the most fruitful part of it all. The in-between is where I have the opportunity to grow into the saint God has made me to be.

Francis, Maxine, Bella, and John are the reason I know I will make it to heaven one day, not because they bring me difficulty to overcome, but because God has given me the grace to love them so deeply that to deny myself and pick up my cross becomes something I want to do. Every time I do, I experience the pure joy of this Easter season: the love of our God and Creator, through Christ, in each other.

Hallelujah, He is RISEN!

Specific Prayer Requests:

- For Francis’s continued progress at Richmond Hope Therapy during his last week, specifically for him to bear weight through his legs and feet for extended periods of time and to initiate transitional movements on his own
- For Francis to have no seizure activity as we begin weaning his final seizure medication
- For all of the therapists and patients at Richmond Hope
- For John and my continued emotional healing and strength
