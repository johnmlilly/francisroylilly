Francis is home! We had a beautiful send off from our NICU family as we departed for home. We will never forget their care and dedication to helping us through everything. We brought him home on Wednesday and have been settling into our new life as a family of five. Bella and Maxine are over the moon and obsessed with their little brother!

We will be sure to continue sharing updates about Francis’ progress and development but they will be less frequent than before. We have many follow-up appointments and therapies ahead of us so please continue to keep us in your prayers!
